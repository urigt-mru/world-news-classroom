Daily Summary Directions

Choose one story from today’s episode.
Write a clear summary that explains the important information from the story. Your response should be accurate, complete, and written in formal academic language.

Model Response

Example: One story from today’s episode explained how a government decision affected people in a specific community. The report described who was involved, what happened, where it took place, and why the issue matters. This story is important because it shows how decisions made by leaders can affect everyday life. It connects to Social Studies because we study how citizens, governments, and communities respond to challenges.
